B.TECH CSE (CYBER SECURITY) — SEMESTER 2 EXAMINATION
CS103: Digital Logic & Computer Architecture (Credits: 3)
Duration: 3 Hours | Maximum Marks: 100
============================================================

COURSE LEARNING OUTCOMES:
  1. Design and analyze combinational and sequential digital circuits
  2. Understand processor architecture and instruction execution models

============================================================
