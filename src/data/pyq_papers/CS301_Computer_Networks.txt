B.TECH CSE (CYBER SECURITY) — SEMESTER 4 EXAMINATION
CS301: Computer Networks (Credits: 4)
Duration: 3 Hours | Maximum Marks: 100
============================================================

COURSE LEARNING OUTCOMES:
  1. Analyze and design network protocols across all OSI layers
